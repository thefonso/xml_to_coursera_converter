Now you try

In this activity, you will use ChatGPT to help gather information on a topic of your choice. Once you’ve completed this activity, return to the lesson to share what you have learned.

  1. Access ChatGPT through the app or website.
  2. Follow the four steps to gather information using ChatGPT:
    1. Determine what you want to learn about.
    2. Prompt ChatGPT to gather information for you.
    3. Review the answer.
    4. Ask for more details (if needed).

If you need ideas for topics to gather information on, try one of the following prompts:

  • Can you give me detailed information about starting a home garden, including tips for beginners?
  • Help me gather information on how to network with my colleagues in my company.
  • Can you provide information on professional development opportunities in my field, including certifications, courses, and workshops?

Once you've completed the activity, take time to reflect on the following questions.

Reflections

  • How did ChatGPT help you gather information on your chosen topic?
  • What key points and details did ChatGPT highlight about your topic? How did these help you better understand the subject?
  • How did the ChatGPT's response to your prompt for more details enhance your understanding of the topic?
