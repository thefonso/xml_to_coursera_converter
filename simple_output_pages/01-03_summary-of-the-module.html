Summary of the module

You will explore the various AI technologies used in customer service and discover how to effectively utilize AI to enhance the customer experience. The two lessons in this module help you understand the impact of AI in the customer service industry.

Modern customer service

  • Explore the common types of AI technologies used in customer service.

Careers

  • Discuss the importance of customer service in different industries.
  • Explore the various career opportunities available in the customer service industry.
